# M2.2 Static Volumetric Representation Spike Report

## Authority and boundary

- M1 sources: **FROZEN / UNCHANGED**
- M2.1 2D offset optical model: **EXHAUSTED / BASELINE ONLY**
- M2: **ACTIVE**
- M3: **BLOCKED**
- No fluid simulation, formation, animation, core, production migration, or Three.js

## Why M2.1 was insufficient

M2.1 sampled one 2D neutral volume at a few UV offsets. Those samples were not different locations along a real viewing ray, so front/back occlusion, view-dependent ray length, and depth accumulation were not represented. M2.2 keeps the M2.1 hero as the fixed baseline and introduces a separate derived volume representation.

## M2.2 representation

The spike derives an 8-slice RGBA atlas from the frozen `neutral-water-volume.png`, `neutral-thickness.png`, and `neutral-water-detail.png`. The slice alpha is authored volume alpha passed through a deterministic front-veil → internal-mass → rear-veil depth envelope. Thickness controls the ray extent in the shader; detail remains a small density/extinction perturbation. No M1 source pixels are rewritten and no generic 3D noise is introduced.

The [Z slice montage](./m2.2-z-slice-montage.png) shows that the eight slices are not identical copies. The HTML spike uses an analytic sphere to calculate entry, exit, and ray length, then samples 8, 12, or 16 positions and accumulates front-to-back with Beer–Lambert-style transmittance.

## Evidence and ablations

The CPU mirror is deterministic evidence for the derived representation, not a GPU benchmark. Its sample A/B is already near convergence: 8→12 MAE `0.0575`, 12→16 MAE `0.0298`. Increasing sample count therefore does not solve the visual defect.

- Depth OFF/ON MAE: `3.2986`; the ON image changes coverage/depth response but does not produce clear internal front/back organization.
- Density OFF/ON MAE: `5.0186`; alpha is genuinely used as mass density, but the source mass is still broadly uniform.
- Thickness OFF/ON MAE: `1.3034`; thickness changes ray extent and attenuation, but does not create authored flow masses.
- Detail OFF/ON MAE: `0.0067`; the frozen detail alpha is too sparse to visibly organize the water volume.

Debug views expose ray length, accumulated density, final transmittance, center depth slice, and the full slice montage. The verification-only WebGL page is [`m2.2-static-volume.html`](./m2.2-static-volume.html).

## Fresh Critic result

Against FINAL_ART_025, FINAL_ART_050, the M2.1 baseline, and the M2.2 CPU hero:

1. M2.2 has a real Z carrier and ray-length calculation, but not a convincing front/back water body.
2. Flat/fog remains.
3. Glass-lens feeling is reduced, not cleared.
4. Cloudy mass occlusion is not legible.
5. The deeper organization in FINAL_ART_050 is absent.
6. Sparse detail remains effectively isolated / invisible.
7. The largest defect is missing authored internal spatial masses in the frozen source package. The frozen detail alpha is especially sparse: mean `0.000501`, nonzero coverage `1.16%`, max `0.431373` in normalized alpha.

## Second structural cycle: geometry carrier G2

The first geometry route still read as two soft spots over a fog field. A second verification-only cycle therefore changed the carrier topology instead of changing sample count: two independently masked opposed curved mass bands, four ordered translucent sheets, a broad central cyan body, and compact cool / warm point fields. The palette remains derived from frozen `neutral-water-volume.rgb`; Final Art PNGs are comparison references only. The new route is [`m2.2-geometry-g2.html`](./m2.2-geometry-g2.html) and the hero is [`m2.2-g2-geometry-spike.png`](./m2.2-g2-geometry-spike.png).

G2 is visibly more structured than the smooth volume and G1 geometry routes: the central body, opposing arcs, and warm/cool roles can be located without reading the debug labels. It still does not reproduce the broad cloudy occlusion and shell highlight hierarchy of FINAL_ART_050. Machine review records `luminanceStdInside=0.0763` against a `0.08` minimum and `p95Gradient=0.00748` against a `0.010` minimum; warm/cool chroma thresholds pass, but the structural gate remains failed. This is a second route attempt, not a material-family PASS.

The GPU verification page also corrected a concrete optical defect found by adversarial review: the final fragment alpha no longer uses a fixed `mask*.90`; it now includes computed `1-trans` coverage and film contribution. Browser runtime remains one pass, zero framebuffer, `shaderErrors=0`. This fixes the evidence path but does not make the current carrier visually sufficient.

## Route review: geometry alternative

The first volumetric construction was not enough to expose the target's internal masses. A verification-only geometry alternative was therefore built without changing M1 or production: an analytic sphere domain with two broad internal mass anchors and four translucent curved flow sheets. Its palette is derived from the frozen neutral volume RGB; the geometry is explicitly marked as a route-review spike.

The geometry spike is visibly more promising than the smooth volume hero because internal mass separation and flow direction are readable. It is still **not accepted** as a material-family PASS: the shell, warm/cool points, and full Final Art organization remain incomplete. This changes the blocker classification from an unproven `SOURCE` claim to **VOLUME_CONSTRUCTION**. The current evidence does not prove that M1 source information is insufficient; it proves that the tested 8-slice construction did not organize it correctly.
