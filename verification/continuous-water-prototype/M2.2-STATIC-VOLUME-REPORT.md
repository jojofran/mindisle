# M2.2 Static Volumetric Representation Spike Report

## Authority and boundary

- M1 sources: **FROZEN / UNCHANGED**
- M2.1 2D offset optical model: **EXHAUSTED / BASELINE ONLY**
- M2: **ACTIVE**
- M3: **BLOCKED**
- No fluid simulation, formation, animation, core, production migration, or Three.js

## Why M2.1 was insufficient

M2.1 sampled one 2D neutral volume at a few UV offsets. Those samples were not different locations along a real viewing ray, so front/back occlusion, view-dependent ray length, and depth accumulation were not represented. M2.2 keeps the M2.1 hero as the fixed baseline and introduces a separate derived volume representation.

## M2.2 representation

The spike derives an 8-slice RGBA atlas from the frozen `neutral-water-volume.png`, `neutral-thickness.png`, and `neutral-water-detail.png`. The slice alpha is authored volume alpha passed through a deterministic front-veil → internal-mass → rear-veil depth envelope. Thickness controls the ray extent in the shader; detail remains a small density/extinction perturbation. No M1 source pixels are rewritten and no generic 3D noise is introduced.

The [Z slice montage](./m2.2-z-slice-montage.png) shows that the eight slices are not identical copies. The HTML spike uses an analytic sphere to calculate entry, exit, and ray length, then samples 8, 12, or 16 positions and accumulates front-to-back with Beer–Lambert-style transmittance.

## Evidence and ablations

The CPU mirror is deterministic evidence for the derived representation, not a GPU benchmark. Its sample A/B is already near convergence: 8→12 MAE `0.0262`, 12→16 MAE `0.0049`. Increasing sample count therefore does not solve the visual defect.

- Depth OFF/ON MAE: `2.5259`; the ON image changes coverage/depth response but does not produce clear internal front/back organization.
- Density OFF/ON MAE: `9.3605`; alpha is genuinely used as mass density, but the source mass is still broadly uniform.
- Thickness OFF/ON MAE: `1.2096`; thickness changes ray extent and attenuation, but does not create authored flow masses.
- Detail OFF/ON MAE: `0.0063`; the frozen detail alpha is too sparse to visibly organize the water volume.

Debug views expose ray length, accumulated density, final transmittance, center depth slice, and the full slice montage. The verification-only WebGL page is [`m2.2-static-volume.html`](./m2.2-static-volume.html).

## Fresh Critic result

Against FINAL_ART_025, FINAL_ART_050, the M2.1 baseline, and the M2.2 CPU hero:

1. M2.2 has a real Z carrier and ray-length calculation, but not a convincing front/back water body.
2. Flat/fog remains.
3. Glass-lens feeling is reduced, not cleared.
4. Cloudy mass occlusion is not legible.
5. The deeper organization in FINAL_ART_050 is absent.
6. Sparse detail remains effectively isolated / invisible.
7. The largest defect is missing authored internal spatial masses in the frozen source package. The frozen detail alpha is especially sparse: mean `0.000501`, nonzero coverage `1.16%`, max `0.431373` in normalized alpha.

## Decision

- M2.2 volumetric representation: **BLOCKED**
- Blocker type: **SOURCE**
- Three.js: **NOT JUSTIFIED**
- READY_FOR_M3: **NO**
- Cycles used: **1**

The spike proves that analytic entry/exit and front-to-back accumulation can run in the verification route. It does not prove that the current frozen source package contains enough spatial information to reproduce the target water family. Do not increase sample count or start a second volume-tuning cycle. Route review must decide whether to reopen the source package for a richer neutral density/flow representation or explicitly choose the geometry alternative.
